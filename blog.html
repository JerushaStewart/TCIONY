Janet Cohen

Janet Cohen is a dedicated professional and dynamic force at TCIONY, where she leverages her extensive experience in the industry to drive innovation and success. With a career spanning over many years, Janet has developed a reputation for her exceptional leadership skills, strategic vision, and unwavering commitment to excellence.

At TCIONY, Janet serves as the Program Director and Board Member, where she oversees many projects having to do with the youth. Her role involves managing teams and developing strategies with Al Cohen. Her expertise in project management and business development gives her a lot of real world experience to teach kids that need to know more about what its like to work at a real job. Her dedication has been instrumental in steering the company towards achieving its goals.

Outside of her professional life, Janet is passionate about her family and her role as a wife, mother and buisness woman. She believes in teaching the next generation. Her dedication extends beyond her role at TCIONY, as she actively encourages the youth to participate at Senior Centers.

Janet Cohen is not just a cornerstone of TCIONY but also a source of inspiration and leadership. Her commitment to excellence and innovation continues to shape the future of TCIONY and the industry.
